HOW IT IS TIMED

The print step turns the 8 ink plates into halftone dots on paper. Its time starts after the plates finish drawing and ends when the printed image is on the page. The WebGL time includes uploading the 8 plates to the graphics chip and waiting for it to finish. Scene is the rest of the frame: drawing the plates and the paper-white marks.

Each frame renders once to warm up, then 3 more times; the tables show the median. Pixel diff is the mean difference between the two prints over red, green and blue, out of 255, with the largest single difference in brackets. It must stay at 2 or below.

Renderer: renderer/riso-window.js. Frames: tools/frames.json.
